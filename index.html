How AurumMY calculates prices

Live Malaysian retailer quotations are displayed as quoted, not automatically replaced by converted global spot rates. Where historical gold-futures and currency records are available, the experimental forecast estimates a short-term percentage movement and applies it to the selected Malaysian quotation. Published daily accuracy is measured using a rolling historical backtest.
