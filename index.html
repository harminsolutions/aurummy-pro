How AurumMY calculates prices

Verified retailer quotations or user-entered local benchmarks are displayed separately from converted global spot values. Where historical gold-futures and currency records are available, the experimental forecast estimates a short-term percentage movement and applies it to the selected Malaysian quotation. Published daily accuracy is measured using a rolling historical backtest.

AURUMMY · IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Before you view gold prices

AurumMY provides indicative gold prices and experimental next-day forecasts for Malaysia and India. It is an information service, not a guaranteed pricing or trading system.

  • Forecasts are uncertain. Actual prices may rise or fall beyond any predicted range. No prediction accuracy or investment return is guaranteed.
  • Market references are not shop quotations. International spot conversions, retailer quotes, and manually entered prices have different meanings. Check the selected source and its update time.
  • Final prices differ. Gold purity, retail margins, buyback spreads, taxes, making charges, and local jewellery-shop policies can affect the price you actually pay or receive.
  • Not financial advice. Always confirm the latest rate directly with the relevant dealer or official source before buying or selling gold.

Historical backtest figures describe past model behaviour only; they do not establish 99% accuracy for tomorrow’s prices.
